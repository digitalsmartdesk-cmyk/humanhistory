Industrial breakthroughs

Twenty inventions and processes that changed how people make things, from flaked stone to connected factories, grouped into four eras.

Ancient foundations 2.6 million years ago to 1440s

  1. c. 2.6 million BCE≈ 2,600,000 yrs ago

    Stone tools

    Early hominins strike flakes from cores to make cutting edges, the first manufactured technology.

    Tool making
  2. c. 400,000 BCE≈ 400,000 yrs ago

    Regular use of fire

    Cooking and warmth first, then the heat later used for pottery kilns and metal smelting.

    Energy
  3. c. 10,000 BCE≈ 12,000 yrs ago

    Agriculture

    Crop farming and animal herding produce food surpluses, permanent settlements, and specialized trades.

    Food
  4. c. 3500 BCE≈ 5,500 yrs ago

    Bronze and the wheel

    Copper-tin alloys give harder tools, and wheeled carts and potter's wheels speed up trade and crafts.

    Materials
  5. c. 1200 BCE≈ 3,200 yrs ago

    Iron smelting

    Iron ore is more common than tin, so tougher, cheaper tools and plows spread widely.

    Materials
  6. c. 105 CE≈ 1,900 yrs ago

    Papermaking

    Plant-fiber paper, traditionally credited to Cai Lun in China, becomes a cheap medium for records and books.

    Information
  7. 1440s≈ 590 yrs ago

    Movable-type printing press

    Gutenberg's press makes books affordable and standardizes the spread of knowledge across Europe.

    Information

Steam and mechanization 1712 to 1830s

  1. 1712 to 1776≈ 315 yrs ago

    Steam engine

    Newcomen's engine pumps mines, and Watt's separate condenser makes steam power efficient enough for factories.

    Energy
  2. 1764 to 1790s≈ 262 yrs ago

    Mechanized textiles

    The spinning jenny, water frame, and power loom move cloth production from homes into mills.

    Factory system
  3. 1825≈ 201 yrs ago

    Steam railways

    The Stockton and Darlington line opens the era of locomotive freight and passenger travel.

    Transport

Steel, electricity, mass production 1856 to 1913

  1. 1856≈ 170 yrs ago

    Bessemer steel process

    Blowing air through molten iron makes steel cheap enough for rails, ships, bridges, and skyscrapers.

    Materials
  2. 1882≈ 144 yrs ago

    Central electric power stations

    Edison's Pearl Street Station in New York and a London plant begin selling electricity to customers, so factories can drop belt-driven shafts.

    Energy
  3. 1886≈ 140 yrs ago

    Automobile

    Benz's Patent-Motorwagen puts the internal combustion engine into a practical road vehicle.

    Transport
  4. 1913≈ 113 yrs ago

    Moving assembly line

    Ford's Highland Park plant cuts Model T assembly from over 12 hours to about 90 minutes.

    Mass production
  5. 1909 to 1913≈ 113 yrs ago

    Haber-Bosch ammonia synthesis

    Fixing nitrogen from air at industrial scale gives the world synthetic fertilizer and supports modern population growth.

    Chemicals

Digital 1947 to today

  1. 1947 to 1958≈ 79 yrs ago

    Transistor and integrated circuit

    Solid-state switches replace vacuum tubes, then get packed onto chips, making computers small and cheap.

    Electronics
  2. 1956≈ 70 yrs ago

    Shipping container

    Malcom McLean's first container voyage standardizes cargo and cuts the cost of moving goods between continents.

    Logistics
  3. 1961≈ 65 yrs ago

    Industrial robot

    The Unimate arm starts work at a General Motors plant, handling hot die-cast parts.

    Automation
  4. 1990s≈ 30 yrs ago

    Commercial internet

    Networked ordering, tracking, and design tools let companies coordinate suppliers worldwide.

    Information
  5. 2010s to now≈ 10 yrs ago

    AI and connected factories

    Sensors and machine learning drive predictive maintenance, quality inspection, and adaptive production planning.

    Automation
