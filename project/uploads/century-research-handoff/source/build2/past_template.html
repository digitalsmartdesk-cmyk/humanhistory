Evidence brief: the record

Record of catastrophes

Nineteen events, from a volcano 74,000 years ago to COVID-19

Natural disasters, plagues, wars, famines and industrial accidents in date order. Each entry gives the scale, the cause, how it spread and what followed, and says how reliable the numbers are.

Figures current to 13 October 2026. Most come from Wikipedia summaries; see the notes on source quality at the end.

Estimated deaths

@@DEATHS@@

Ranges are the lowest and highest estimates in the sources. The Atlantic slave trade bar adds deaths at sea to deaths inside Africa after capture, which is my arithmetic. The Americas, Toba, Tambora and the Bronze Age collapse have no death totals that I could confirm, so they are left out of this chart. Smallpox covers a whole century.

Share of a population lost

@@SHARES@@

The bases differ, so read each label. A region, a city, a country and the world are not the same denominator. The Bengal and COVID-19 shares are my arithmetic, using about 60.3 million people in Bengal and about 7.8 billion people in the world.
