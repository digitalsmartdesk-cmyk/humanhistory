<h2 id="limits">What this chapter cannot show</h2>
<ul class="plain"><li>The three snapshot dates are loose. Many sources give 1900, 1920, 1950 or 2016 instead of 1825, 1925 and 2025, and each cell names the year it uses.</li><li>The household technology series is for the United States only. I found no matching world series for the same machines.</li><li>The 1825 column is the thinnest. For most rows I did not find a figure for that date, and I have left it blank rather than estimate.</li><li>Dress is described for Western fashion only and I have no 2025 description at all.</li><li>Wikipedia and OWID pages were read through a summarizing tool, and a few values come from chart data files rather than page text. Check the figures against the originals before relying on them.</li><li>A statement of appearance, such as a skirt or a skyline, cannot be reduced to a figure. These tables show what can be counted, not how life felt.</li></ul>
